A Next.js 15 App Router e-commerce, POS, and operations platform for a Nigerian red palm oil producer with a mill in Iwo, Osun State.

**Tech stack:** Prisma & PostgreSQL, Paystack payments, Cloudinary media storage, Resend transactional emails, and JWT admin authentication.
