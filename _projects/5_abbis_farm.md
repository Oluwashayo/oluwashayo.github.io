Abbis Farm is an integrated enterprise commerce, point-of-sale (POS), and supply chain platform built for a commercial red palm oil processing and distribution business headquartered in Iwo, Osun State, Nigeria. The application unifies customer-facing retail commerce with on-site mill production tracking and wholesale dispatch.

Engineered with Next.js 15 and the React App Router, the platform features a full-stack TypeScript architecture powered by Prisma ORM and PostgreSQL. The system integrates Paystack for multi-currency card and bank transfer checkout, Cloudinary for optimized media management, and Resend for automated transactional order receipts and customer communications.

The operational backbone includes an offline-resilient POS interface for retail mill transactions, dynamic inventory reconciliation across bulk drums and retail packaging, and role-based administrative dashboards secured via JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
