The Fleet Management Platform is an operational logistics and asset management system engineered to manage government vehicle fleets, requisition pipelines, and maintenance lifecycles. Built to eliminate unauthorized vehicle usage and track operational expenditures, the solution integrates hardware tracking with administrative oversight.

The system pairs a responsive administrative web dashboard with a cross-platform mobile client for drivers and dispatchers. Key capabilities include real-time vehicle allocation, fuel consumption logs, route tracking, and incident reporting across active department fleets.

A core architectural feature is a multi-tiered, two-level requisition and approval workflow. Trip requests initiated by departmental officers require sequential clearance from administrative officers and fleet directors, enforcing strict accountability and auditability for public assets.
